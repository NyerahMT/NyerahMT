## About

I work in **nuclear fabrication** and spend much of my free time pulling apart software, simulations, mechanical systems, and anything else interesting enough to understand from the inside out.

My development work tends to sit around the ugly parts of a system:

`Platform ports` · `Rendering` · `Physics` · `Performance` · `Input` · `GPU compute` · `Debugging`

I'm especially interested in software that has to represent or interact with something physical.
